# Sweep Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Sweep is made by Anoushka Saboo ("we"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use Sweep.

## Your photos
Sweep needs access to your photo library so you can review, keep and delete photos. Your photos and videos are processed only on your device. We never upload, copy or see them. Photos are deleted only when you confirm in Apple's own deletion prompt, and deleted items go to your Recently Deleted album for 30 days.

## Information stored on your device
Sweep keeps the following only on your device: which photos you have already reviewed, your stats (space freed, items deleted, streak), colour information calculated from your photos, and cached place names. Deleting the app removes it.

## Place names
To show town and country names in Search, Sweep sends the approximate location stored with your photos (not the photos themselves) to Apple's geocoding service. Apple's privacy policy applies to that request: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

## Advertising (free version)
The free version shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect device information such as an advertising identifier where permitted, IP address, device type and ad interaction data to serve and measure ads. Sweep does not ask to track you across other companies' apps, so ads are not personalised using that permission. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent through Google's consent form, and you can change your choice any time from "Ad privacy choices" in the app. See how Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

Sweep Pro subscribers see no ads, and the ad SDK is not started for them.

## Purchases
Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

## Children
Sweep is not directed at children under 13.
