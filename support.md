# Sweep Support

Need help with Sweep: Photo Cleaner Swipe? Email **unreasonablelabs@gmail.com** and we'll get back to you.

## Common questions

**Are my photos uploaded anywhere?**
No. Sweep sorts, groups and analyses your photos on your iPhone. They never leave your device.

**I deleted a photo by mistake.**
Deleted photos go to the Photos app's Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Open Photos, then Albums, then Recently Deleted, and recover it.

**Sweep can only see some of my photos.**
Open Settings, then Sweep, then Photos, and choose Full Access. Or tap "Select more" on Sweep's home screen.

**How do I restore Sweep Pro on a new phone?**
Open Sweep, go to Sweep Pro, and tap Restore purchases while signed in to the same Apple Account.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Sweep Pro. Or use "Manage subscription" inside the app.

**Ad privacy choices**
Free users in regions that require it can change ad consent from "Ad privacy choices" on the home screen.
